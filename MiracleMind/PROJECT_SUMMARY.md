## 🎯 Project Overview

A fully functional, educational website that teaches visitors the mindset, problem-solving strategies, manufacturing excellence, and innovation methods used by China and USA to become economic superpowers.

**Goal**: Help people learn how to find solutions, build tools, and master manufacturing by understanding the secrets behind two of the world's most successful economies.
